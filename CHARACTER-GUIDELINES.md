# Character Guidelines: Momotamu & Vivi

Momotamu and Vivi are SparkleSnap characters. Original application **code**, including motion and animation logic, is MIT-licensed (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), with the Apache-2.0 component exceptions in [NOTICE](NOTICE). The characters themselves — their designs, artwork, sprite sheets, bundled reference recordings and illustrated animation frames — are shared under these guidelines, in the spirit of characters like Hatsune Miku and the Touhou cast: **you are welcome to use them, as long as you credit their home and treat them kindly.**

## What these guidelines cover

- The Momotamu and Vivi characters: their canonical names, spoken aliases, looks, voice references, and personalities.
- Their artwork and illustrated animation frames in `characters/momotamu/` and `characters/vivi/`, plus the selected reference WAVs in `assets/voice/`.
- The two stage textures in `characters/momotamu-presence/assets/gen/` use these same credited, noncommercial art terms, as selected by the owner on 2026-10-24.
- External model weights and hosted-provider voices retain their respective upstream terms. See the [asset inventory guide](docs/ASSET-PROVENANCE.md) for creation evidence, VRoid tool origins, and audio logs.

## Identity & canonical aliases

Both companions have official spoken nicknames recognized by the voice routing engine and live prompt orchestration:

* **Momotamu (✿):** Canonical name **Momotamu** · Spoken nicknames **Tammy**, *Momo*. A bright-hearted, attentive companion whose curiosity brings expansive, thoughtful perspectives to every conversation. Her dress was modeled in VRoid Studio inspired by nadeflat's design on BOOTH (see [Asset Provenance](docs/ASSET-PROVENANCE.md)).
* **Vivi (✧):** Canonical name **Vivi** · Spoken nicknames **Vivian**, *Viv*. A punchy, quick-witted companion who identifies concrete points quickly and asks the sharp questions that move discussions forward. Her off-the-shoulder outfit was modeled in VRoid Studio inspired by 卯デザインラボ on BOOTH (see [Asset Provenance](docs/ASSET-PROVENANCE.md)).

The SparkleSnap/Duo names, logos and app icons are covered separately [below](#the-sparklesnap-brand).

## You may — freely, without asking

- **Make fan works.** Draw them, animate them, write about them, feature them in videos, streams, games, and mods.
- **Use this app's art in your fork or remix** of Duo Voice Harness, or in another free, non-commercial research project.
- **Share** what you make, online or in print, for free.

## Please always

- **Credit their home.** For example: *Momotamu & Vivi © SparkleSnap — sparklesnap.dev*. In a video description, a README, an About screen, or the end of a page is fine.
- **Make it clear it's yours.** A fan work or fork should not look like an official SparkleSnap release. Call it fan-made, a fork, or a remix, and give it a name of your own.
- **Keep them kind.** Momotamu and Vivi are warm, thoughtful companions, and anyone of any age should be able to enjoy talking with them.

## Please don't

- Put them in sexual, hateful, violent, or shocking content.
- Use them for political campaigns, scams, phishing, or anything meant to deceive.
- Claim them as your own characters, or register their names or looks as trademarks or designs.
- Sell them, or products featuring them, without asking first. This includes merchandise, paid apps, NFTs, and stock-asset packs.

## Commercial use or collaboration?

Commercial use, collaborations, or inquiries: reach out via [sparklesnap.dev](https://sparklesnap.dev/).

## The SparkleSnap brand

The SparkleSnap/Duo names, watermark, logos and app icons may only be used to credit or link to SparkleSnap. This includes `assets/brand/`, `assets/icon.ico`, `assets/icon-256.png` and `assets/sparklesnap-google-logo-v1.jpg`. In a fork, replace these brand marks with your own mark.

These guidelines describe the project's declared reuse policy. See [Artwork, voices and reuse](docs/ASSET-PROVENANCE.md) for complete provenance records, asset inventory details, and creator attributions.
